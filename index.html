The method

A probability, a fair price, a plan.

Every call is a disagreement with the market price, sized to your bankroll and tracked whether it wins or loses.

01

Price it ourselves

Surface, form and fatigue go into the model first. The bookmaker's number comes after, so it can't anchor the read.

02

Take the value

The gap between fair price and market price sets the stake. No gap, no bet, and most matches get no call at all.

03

Track it in the open

Wins and losses go into the same record, where members can see them.

Questions

What people ask first.

What exactly do I get?

Every call, pushed to Telegram the moment the odds are out: the match, the bookmaker, the price and the stake as a percentage of your bank. Plus tournament previews and the results tracked in the open.

Is WaveHub a bookmaker?

No. We publish analysis. We take no bets and hold no money. You place your bets with your own bookmaker.

Do I need to be experienced?

No. Every call tells you what to take, at what price, with how much of your bank, and why. Betting Masterclass adds a staking plan and the full course library.

Why only ATP tennis?

One tour followed properly beats ten followed badly. The model is built on ATP matches, surfaces and schedules, so that is the only place we price.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel before your membership renews and your access runs until the end of the term you paid for.
