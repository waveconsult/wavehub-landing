The method

No tipster theatre. A probability, a fair price, a plan.

Every call is a disagreement with the market price, sized to your bankroll and tracked whether it wins or loses.

STEP 01

Price it ourselves

Surface, form and fatigue go into the model first. The book's number is only looked at afterwards, so it cannot anchor the read.

STEP 02

Take the value

The gap between fair price and market price sets the stake. No gap, no bet, and most matches get no call at all.

STEP 03

Track it in the open

Wins and losses go into the same record, where members can see them.

What exactly do I get?

Every call, pushed the moment the odds are out: the match, the book, the price and the stake as a percentage of your bank. Plus tournament previews and the results tracked in the open.

Is WaveHub a bookmaker?

No. We publish analysis. We do not accept bets, we do not settle them and we never hold your money. You place everything yourself, at your own book, in your own name.

How do I know the record is real?

Every position is timestamped at posting, before the line moves, and settled publicly whether it wins or loses. The complete ledger exports to CSV — request it before you pay and check it yourself.

Do I need to be experienced?

The card works at any size, but the variance is uncomfortable under about 100 units. Most members start between €3,000 and €10,000 and stake 0.5–2% per position.

Why only ATP tennis?

Because depth on one tour beats a thin read on four. Sixty-plus tournaments a year is already more than most people can follow properly, and a market you visit twice a month is a market you do not know.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, and the season's ledger stays accessible to you. Both tiers run for twelve months and renew automatically at the end of the term. Cancel before the renewal date and nothing further is charged.
